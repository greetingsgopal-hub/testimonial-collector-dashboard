1. Executive Summary & Product Introduction

The PandaPraise Chrome Extension is an enterprise browser extension engineered to capture, verify, and deploy customer social proof with zero friction.

Traditional testimonial software (like Senja.io) was built for the Silicon Valley tech bubble, focusing predominantly on Twitter/X, Product Hunt, and email forms. However, in India and global emerging markets, over 90% of business deals, client approvals, freelancer feedbacks, and D2C customer praises occur inside WhatsApp Web, validated by UPI payment confirmations.

PandaPraise converts your web browser into a bi-directional trust engine:

  • Inbound Capture: 1-click praise clipping directly from WhatsApp Web chats, Twitter/X feeds, and LinkedIn recommendations straight into your private Proof Vault.
  • Outbound Conversion: An instant sales objection solver allowing founders and sales reps to search their testimonial database and inject verified social proof into active WhatsApp or LinkedIn chats in under 2 seconds.

3. Core Capabilities & Feature Breakdown

💬 1. Native WhatsApp Web In-Chat Clipper

Monitors web.whatsapp.com chat streams. Hovering over any incoming client message reveals a subtle 🐼 Clip button. Clicking it instantly extracts the contact's name or phone, message body, and timestamp.

💳 2. Automated UPI & Transaction Proof Verification

Detects financial markers (₹, INR, UTR, UPI, GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, credited). It auto-checks the Verified UPI Proof badge and attaches cryptographic transaction tags to eliminate skepticism of fake reviews.

🪄 3. 1-Click Hinglish <-> Global English AI Polisher

Tapping 🪄 Hinglish Polish recognizes vernacular phrases ("ekdum top class", "paisa vasool", "dil khush ho gaya") and produces an international corporate version ("Top-tier service delivering exceptional value and results"). You can toggle back to original anytime.

⚡ 4. Sales Vault Search & 1-Click "Drop into Chat"

Search all stored testimonials by keyword, client name, or tag. Clicking 💬 Drop in Chat automatically executes direct DOM insertion into WhatsApp Web's or LinkedIn's message composer.

🔄 5. Zero-Touch Auto-Auth Sync

Whenever you are logged into pandapraise.com/dashboard, the extension automatically pairs your active project ID. No manual copy-pasting of API keys is required.

4. Step-by-Step Installation Manual (10 Seconds)

1Open Extension Settings: In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions in Microsoft Edge).
2Enable Developer Mode: Toggle the Developer mode switch in the top-right corner to ON.
3Load Unpacked Extension: Click "Load unpacked" in the top-left corner and select the directory:
testimonial-collector-dashboard\extension
4Pin & Auto-Sync: Click the puzzle icon in your browser toolbar and pin PandaPraise. Open your dashboard at https://pandapraise.com/dashboard and your account will auto-pair!
