## 2. Prospect Qualification Criteria

Before adding a prospect to this tracker, verify they meet all **Tier 1 criteria**:
- [ ] Currently in business with paying customers/clients in the last 60 days.
- [ ] Has an active, live website or portfolio that they control.
- [ ] Has positive customer feedback trapped in unstructured formats (Slack, email, WhatsApp, DMs, Google Docs).
- [ ] The contact person is the primary decision maker / founder with direct purchasing authority.
- [ ] Not a personal friend, not a family account, and not an internal testing account.
